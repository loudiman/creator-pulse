<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `pluralization` — `creators.yaml` introduces a `sources:` map, so one creator carries many
platforms rather than one.

**Primary noun:** `creator`, identified by the hand-written `creator_id` slug. The platform is a
*detail of one variant* of that creator, not a peer identity.

**Decision:** `promote`. The generalized identity is `creator`, and `(creator_id, source)` is the
composite key every later phase reads. `creator_id` is promoted to the primary noun (D-10), the
platform demoted to a key inside the `sources:` map (D-09). This is what lets Phase 6's
`/creator <name>` roll one person up across YouTube, Twitch and TikTok without a join the config
does not express.

**Not add-alongside.** A flat list of platform/identifier pairs, or top-level grouping by platform,
would keep the platform primary and make the per-person rollup a join. Both alternatives were
considered and rejected in D-09.
</assumption_delta_decision>
