## Phase Goal

> The Sheet stops being a dump and becomes a two-way surface — it formats itself and talks back to Discord.

*(Reproduced verbatim from ROADMAP. Not in user-story form; see 05-01's note.)*

<objective>
The phase's paperwork and its one irreplaceable human deliverable.

Two things happen here. The first is ordinary: `COVERAGE.md` records what of Discord's webhook
surface this project integrates and what it deliberately does not, `05-UAT.md` collects the
human-observed evidence that is the *only* proof this phase has, and one stale metadata row in
REQUIREMENTS.md is brought into agreement with the amendment the other three files already carry.

The second is not ordinary and is the reason this plan is not optional. ROADMAP criterion 5 —
"the author can walk someone through the `onEdit` trigger's event object and the webhook call from
memory" — is unchanged by the ownership amendment, and D-03 makes it the control that replaces
hand-typing the code. It closes when the author writes that explanation into `05-UAT.md` in their
own words, unaided, after a walkthrough. **This plan therefore ships the criterion-5 section as
questions with no answers in it**, so there is nothing in the repo to copy from, and the walkthrough
is delivered live in the checkpoint rather than committed as a document for the same reason. An
explanation the agent supplied would prove nothing, and the interview is roughly six hours later.

Output: `COVERAGE.md`, `05-UAT.md`, one corrected table row, and an author who can explain the code
cold.

**Not in this plan:** any change to `apps-script/`, any Python, any systemd unit, any Discord
Developer Portal work. This plan produces documentation, which is precisely why it can state
findings about `Code.gs` without being able to alter them.
</objective>
